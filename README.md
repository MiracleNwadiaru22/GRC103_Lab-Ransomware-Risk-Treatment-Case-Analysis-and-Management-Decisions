# GRC 103 – Ransomware Risk Treatment Case Lab
A practical GRC assignment focused on assessing, prioritising, and treating cybersecurity risks using a fictional healthcare organisation, Northbridge Community Health, based on lessons from the 2021 Ireland Health Service Executive (HSE) ransomware attack.

## Key Areas Covered
- **Risk Assessment:** Identified and prioritised four major risks: service disruption, sensitive-data loss, supplier exposure, and delayed detection.
- **Risk Treatment:** Developed an avoidance and mitigation strategy covering supplier access, MFA, backup protection, managed detection, and staff awareness.
- **Financial Exposure:** Calculated retained financial losses under different cyber insurance claim scenarios.
- **Change Management:** Revised the treatment portfolio following a failed restoration test and an increase in MFA implementation costs.
- **Implementation Planning:** Developed a 30-day treatment plan with assigned owners, budgets, and target dates.
- **Residual Risk & Acceptance:** Evaluated residual risks and proposed time-limited risk acceptance arrangements.
- **Board Reporting:** Prepared a board memo outlining recommended actions, budget requirements, and governance oversight.

## Key Outcome
Developed a revised cybersecurity risk treatment portfolio valued at **$98,000**, within the organisation's $100,000 budget, with a focus on business continuity, patient data protection, and incident response.

## Tools & Deliverables
- Risk Register
- Risk Scoring and Residual Risk Assessment
- Financial Exposure Analysis
- Risk Treatment Plan
- 30-Day Implementation Roadmap
- Board Communication Memo
